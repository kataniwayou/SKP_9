### StepRole in one paragraph (spec approved)
`RunPosition` (`entry` on a fire's dispatch, `terminal` on an outcome nothing accepts) is removed entirely and replaced by `StepRole` = a step's role in a workflow's graph: `entry` (in `entryStepIds`), `terminal` (no successors), `intermediate` (the rest). BaseApi computes it per workflow at start and stores `skp:wf:{w}:step:{s}` → `role`; a dropped step's key is deleted at the next start (unconditionally; kept on stop). The orchestrator stamps it at exactly two points, both resolved from that key: the scheduler's "dispatched an entry step" record, and the one record per returned outcome (`OutcomeTemplates.BranchEnds` / `.Advanced`), regardless of result (a cancelled intermediate step is `intermediate`). Kibana pie: fires that entered in range (`INLINE STATS`), then their outcome records by role (inner ring) and step name (outer ring) — a funnel. The Analyst's `run-boundaries` panel becomes the same funnel; the `ends-not-recorded` replay scenario is deleted. Verified on dev with stand-in data: 15/125/107/89/53/106/106/106/54/54 matches the answer key exactly. **Unverified:** Lens drawing two rings over ES|QL (plan Task 1 spike; fallback treemap).
